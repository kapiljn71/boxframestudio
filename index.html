Who We Are

Box Frame Studio LLP

A Bengaluru-based interior design and manufacturing company delivering spaces that are functional, elegant, and built for modern lifestyles.

BOX FRAME STUDIO LLP combines creative design, precision manufacturing, and seamless execution. We provide complete turnkey interior solutions — from concept development and space planning to manufacturing, delivery, and installation.
